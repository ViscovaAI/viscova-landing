Our own product, in the lab.

Alongside client work we're building a security layer for AI-built software. It checks an app before it ships and keeps watching after.

Why it exists

  • 1,000+

    AI-built apps crawled in our research — the large majority carried a real, exploitable flaw.

  • ₹250 Cr

    Maximum penalty per breach instance under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act.

  • 12%

    Of founders who’d built with AI had ever checked their app for security.

How it decides

  • Deterministic verdicts — a model never gets a vote on whether you’re safe.
  • Every finding points to an observable evidence record, never just “trust us”.
  • Fixes are verified by re-scanning, not suggested and forgotten.
  • Re-scans on every deploy — not a one-time report gathering dust.
  • Built for founders with no engineering team, not another developer-only tool.
